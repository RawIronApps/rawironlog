Common topics

How do I create a workout?

Open the Workouts area and create a workout using the name you want. RawIron Log is designed without required workout templates, so you can build workouts around your own training.

Can I create multiple workouts and exercises?

Yes. The app is designed to let you create workouts with any names and add the exercises you need without imposing a fixed template.

Can I reuse an existing workout?

Use the cloning functionality to make a copy of an existing workout, then customize the copy without changing the original.

Does RawIron Log require an internet connection?

No internet connection is required for the app's core workout-logging functions. External links such as this support page open in your device's web browser when you choose to use them.

How do I back up my data?

Use the Backup & Restore section in Settings to export and restore your workout data. Keep important backup files in a location you control.

How do purchases work?

Paid entitlements are processed through the applicable app store. RawIron Log can recognize a qualifying lifetime entitlement after a normal purchase or an applicable promotional offer. Use the app's purchase/restore controls when you need to restore an eligible purchase.
