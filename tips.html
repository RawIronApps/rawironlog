RAWIRON LOG

Tips & Features

What RawIron Log can do, and where to find it. Many of the most useful features are a swipe or a tap away.

Workouts Workout Timer Exercises Sets Calculator History & Notes Your Data

Workouts

Repeat a workout
Swipe right on a workout and tap Clone. Your last weights and reps appear in each set as a guide.
Workout notes
Tap Note on a workout to write down anything about the session.
Status at a glance
Badges show each workout and exercise as Planned, In Progress, Partial, or Complete.

Workout Timer

Time your workout
Tap Start Workout on a workout's exercises. A banner on every page shows the time; tap it to jump back.
Move to the next exercise
When every set is done, tap Next at the bottom of the Sets page, or Finish Workout after the last one.
Pick up where you left off
Stopped before finishing? Tap Restart Workout to resume the timer from when you started.

Exercises

Every kind of exercise
When adding an exercise, mark it as alternating (left and right reps), body weight, or a timed hold.
Rep range presets
Pick a common rep range in one tap. Change the presets in Settings.
Reorder
Tap Reorder, drag exercises or sets into place, then tap Done.

Sets

Warm-up sets
Swipe right on a set to mark it as a warm-up. Warm-ups are skipped by progressive overload.
Progressive overload
Turn it on in Settings to raise the weight when you clone after reaching the top of your rep range. Tap the trending-up icon on a set to see what you did before.
Past notes
Tap Past Notes to see your notes from the last 5 times you did this exercise or workout.

Calculator

Plate calculator
Tap the calculator icon on the Sets page to see the plates for each side. Change Bar / Base for machines like a leg press.
Estimated 1RM
Switch to 1RM for your estimated max and a percentage table. Tap a row to see its plates.
Your bar and plates
Set your usual bar and the plates you have in Settings.

History & Notes

Exercise history
Pick an exercise on the History tab to see every set you've logged. Swipe right on an entry to clone it into a workout.
All your notes in one place
The Notes tab lists every note, newest first. Filter by type and days, and tap Open to go to its workout or exercise.
Search
Use the search in the title bar to find workouts, exercises, and notes.

Your Data

Training journal
In Settings, tap Create next to Training Journal and pick a date range. It starts with your totals and best sets, then every workout with its sets and notes. Print it, or save it as a PDF.
Back up your data
Export everything to a file in Settings at any time, and restore it whenever you need to.
Pounds or kilograms
Switch units in Settings. Each unit keeps its own bar and plates.

Still have a question?

The in-app version of this guide is in Settings → About & Support → Tips & Features. For anything else, visit Support.
